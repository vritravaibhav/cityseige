The five rules

Everything else in this handbook follows from these.

Town Hall level is your city tier

No other building can go above the Town Hall's level. The Town Hall itself goes to 12.

Every Town Hall level unlocks something

At least one new building, plus higher build limits, more land, a bigger police force and a bigger raid bounty. Some levels also add labour.

Every building type has a limit

How many you may own depends on your Town Hall. The shop counts what's placed and what's in your inventory, so you can't stockpile past it.

Building takes real time

Jobs double in length every level, up to {{MAXT}}, and keep running with the game closed. So do your producers. Gems finish a job early.

Each job needs a free labourer

Labour Huts provide labourers, but the Town Hall caps how many can work: {{LAB}} from Town Hall 1 to 12.

The formulas

These are the actual algorithms. "Base" is the building's level-1 value, shown on every card below. L is the level.

Health

HP = base × (1 + {{HP_PCT}} × (L − 1))

A straight line. Barriers (Roadblock, Spike Trap) grow {{BARRIER_PCT}} per level instead, because they start very tough.

Laser Obelisk: {{OBELISK}}.

Upgrade cost

cost of level L = shop price × {{COSTX}}^(L − 1)

Each level costs {{COSTX}}× the one before, for cash, iron and wood alike.

Town Hall: {{THCOST}}.

Build time

seconds = buildBase × 2^(L − 1), capped at {{MAXT}}

Doubles every level. Most buildings hit the cap around level 10–11.

Town Hall: {{THTIME}}.

Finishing early

<1 min: 1 gem · 1 hour: {{G1H}} · 8 hours: {{GMAX}} · 24 hours: {{G24H}}

Gems scale with the time left, so gemming a short job is cheap and a long one is not.

You start with {{START_GEMS}} gems. The only way to earn more is winning raids ({{GEM_LOW}} at Town Hall 1, {{GEM_HIGH}} at Town Hall 12).

Production

rate × (1 + {{PROD_PCT}} × (L − 1)) · storage × (1 + {{CAP_PCT}} × (L − 1))

Producers fill while the game is open and closed, and stop when full. Storage holds hours of output, so check in a few times a day.

The Crypto Vault is the exception: it can't be tapped and only pays out when a raid breaks it.

Turrets

range +{{T_RANGE}}/L · damage +{{T_DMG}}/L · interval −{{T_RATE}}/L (floor {{T_FLOOR}})

Levels mostly buy a gun HP and reach; damage grows slowly, and some turrets grow it faster (see each card). Every round lands, so each gun you add adds its damage. A parked raider tuned to your Town Hall survives your whole kill box for {{KILL_MIN}}–{{KILL_MAX}}s.

Auras multiply on top: Solar (rate), Fusion (damage), Orbital Relay (range + damage). The same aura twice doesn't stack.

Build limits

allowed = BUILD_LIMITS[building][Town Hall]

A hand-set table, not a formula: 36 buildings × 12 Town Halls. Shown on every card and in full under Master tables.

Roadblocks: {{RB1}} at TH1, {{RB12}} at TH12.

Labour

labourers = min(Labour Huts built, Town Hall cap)

A hut past the cap does nothing until you upgrade the Town Hall.

Cap by Town Hall: {{LAB}}.

Roads

off the asphalt: -{{ONROAD}} top speed, -{{ONROADA}} acceleration

Roads are the fast lanes - for the raider and for your police alike. Leave them out and everyone slows down.

The road budget grows every Town Hall, so shaping the route is a real defensive lever.

Taking damage

guns always land · contact buffer {{IMM}}s · hop every {{HOP}}s

Turret fire, beams, splash and traps always deal their damage, so more guns really is more pressure. Only a police or SWAT contact leaves a brief buffer. The free hop stops you getting stuck but cannot keep you airborne (at most {{HOPAIR}} of a raid).

Landmines inside {{MINEWIN}}s of each other do not all fire, and a building razed by another building's explosion pays only {{CHAINLOOT}} of its loot.

Your gun

autocannon {{CANNON}} per shell ({{CANNONU}} vs police)

The autocannon is what actually razes a city, and the Weapons Lab is the only thing that grows it - by level 12 it hits for {{CANNON12}}, keeping pace with building health.

Cards are burst damage; the cannon is the clock.

Land

build radius: {{R1}} tiles at TH1 → {{R12}} at TH12

One tile is 5.5m. The city wall sits at 15 tiles, so each Town Hall opens a new ring of land inside it.

Anything already beyond your radius stays; you just can't place new things there yet.

How a raid works

You raid your own city with the buggy. Your defenses fight back, and whatever you destroy pays out as loot.

  1. Recon. The camera frames your three gates. Tap one to choose where to break in.
  2. Breach. The buggy rams the gate. Sirens start. Every turret, trap, spawner and aura arms at this moment, at its current level.
  3. Combat. The buggy ({{BUGGY}} stock) auto-fires its cannon. Gates shoot back ({{GATE}}). A police cruiser or SWAT unit touching you is an instant bust. Drones strafe you even mid-air. Traps fire when you drive over them (your rounds fly over them). Inside an EMP field your ability cards are jammed.
  4. Hopping. Space is a free small hop with a {{HOP}}s cooldown, so you can always get over a barrier but can't stay airborne. After a ram or a blast, further rams and blasts do nothing for {{IMM}}s; gunfire always lands.
  5. Lives. {{LIVES}} lives per raid, plus up to 2 spares bought in the garage. Losing all armor or getting busted costs a life, and the police redeploy.
  6. Result. Destroying 100% wins. Gates, trees and traps don't count toward the 100%. You can retreat at any time and keep what you've looted.
Loot per destroyed building: {{LOOT}}. Trees and landmines pay nothing. A Crypto Vault adds everything it has mined.
Gem bounty on a win pays in full only for a real fight: the city must have enough buildings and every kind of defense its Town Hall allows, each with {{KINDSHARE}} of its build limit standing at the Town Hall's level. Every gun, pursuit base (police, SWAT, drones), trap and aura building (Solar Array, Fusion Reactor, EMP Disrupter, Orbital Relay, Quantum Citadel) is its own kind, and every kind weighs the same (TH1: {{MINT1}} targets and {{KINDS1}} kind of defense; TH12: {{MINT12}} targets and {{KINDS12}} kinds). Short of either, the bounty is scaled down: a Town Hall 12 city with its EMPs, Relays and Citadel stowed pays {{GEMS12AURA}} of {{GEM_HIGH}} gems, and with its traps stowed too {{GEMS12TRAPS}}. Stowing everything and raiding an empty city pays nothing.

Saving & timers

Three things are saved in the browser, separately.

Save keyHoldsWhen it saves
city_siege_cityEvery building's type, tile, level and rotation; every road tile; stored output; every running build job as an end time, not a countdown.Within a second of any change, and when the tab is hidden or closed.
city_siege_ecoCash, iron, wood, gems, the Construction Inventory, the level of each stowed building, spare lives.On every spend, collect or purchase.
city_siege_garageTuning levels, card unlocks and levels, the raid deck.On every garage purchase.
Jobs are saved as end times, so they keep counting down while the game is closed. On the next launch, finished jobs show as "finished while you were away" and producers catch up on what they made. A save that won't load is kept as <key>.bak instead of being overwritten. Jobs never complete mid-raid; they wait until you're back in the city.

Known gaps

Things deliberately not done yet, for you to decide on.

  • Main Gates are fixed at 3. They sit on the wall and can't be bought, placed or upgraded. Making them placeable needs a rule for where a raid can start.
  • The original 16 buildings only have 3 looks. Their stats grow to level 12, but their model stops changing after level 3. The 18 new buildings change look through level 6.
  • Touch driving controls don't work. The mobile buttons point at page elements that don't exist, so raids need a keyboard. Unrelated to progression and untouched.
